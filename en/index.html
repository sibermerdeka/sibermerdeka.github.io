Main Page

Welcome to sibermerdeka! Here is where the world of the virtual meets the real, and here you can meet others who are interested in the cyber too...

We are a hackerspace based in Pudu, Kuala Lumpur. Members meet every week on Tuesday night at our hackerspace, but we are available most days. We talk about various topics within this cyber era, among them:

  • Computer networks and the World Wide Web
  • Computers and their power and potential to influence society
  • Public and private data safety and privacy and its place in our lives.

On top of this, we have some facilities for use including:

  • 1 Gbps internet for use :D
  • Computers, monitors, and a printrr for messing around with!
  • Soldering irons and electronic equipment for working on your own projects!
  • Communal DJ controller (Pioneer DDJ 400) to mess around or test for your sets
  • Soundsystem (Speakers across two floors + channel balancer w/ mic input)
  • Game consoles (Switch, PS1, PS2) and boardgames
  • Kitchen (induction stove, air fryer, water filter)
  • Shower with a heater!
  • Lounge for kicking back and relaxing in
  • Fancy rooftop access with a skyline of KL

Interested? We are based on the 4th floor of the shoplot at 392C, Jalan Pudu, and share physical space with Petra Pudu Rooftop Cafe. We are across the road from Pudu LRT station on the Ampang / Sri Petaling Lines.

For more information, contact us at hello (at) sibermerdeka.net, or message us at Instagram.
